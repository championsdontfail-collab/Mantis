# Irregular Verbs Championship — design concept

Dedicated to **Yasya, Kate, Ivan and his friends**.

## Approved visual direction (concept sketch, 21 October 2026)
- Whimsical fairytale forest with a cheerful red squirrel guide.
- Rich illustrated woodland backdrop, mushrooms, acorns, wooden signboards, parchment panels, lush greenery, bright green rounded buttons.
- Mobile-first, readable in Telegram's in-app browser.
- Home screen headline: **Irregular Verbs Championship**; subtitle: **for Yasya, Kate, Ivan and his friends**.
- Squirrel mascot welcomes players, offers hints and celebrates answers.
- Screens: Home, Choose a Mode, Question, Correct Answer, Game Finished, Leaderboard, Your Progress, Settings.
- Modes: Championship (20 questions, 60 seconds), Practice (untimed), Weak Verbs.
- Leaderboard: This Week / All Time, player avatars, scores; names in concept are illustrative, not actual results.
- Progress: recent results chart, strong verbs and verbs needing practice.
- Settings: timer, questions per game, sound, explanations, leaderboard.
- Copy: “Learn · Play · Improve Together!” and “Play with your friends and climb to the top!”
- Gameplay: choose past simple / past participle answers; offer explanations, correct forms, score and retry.

## Implementation status
This is a **design specification**, not an implementation of the illustrated UI. The prototype in `index.html` currently uses a simpler design and local-only scores. A shared leaderboard requires a backend/database. The generated concept illustration is in the ChatGPT conversation; the GitHub text-file connector cannot upload binary images.
